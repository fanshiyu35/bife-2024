Inclusions

What's Included

Full Registration

  • Access to all three days of technical sessions
  • All keynote lectures and industry panel
  • USB drive with full IEEE Xplore proceedings
  • Welcome reception (Nov 14)
  • Gala dinner with awards (Nov 15)
  • All coffee breaks and lunches
  • Conference badge and IEEE certificate of participation
  • Two workshop slots (Nov 16, subject to capacity)

Student Registration

  • Same technical access as full registration
  • Doctoral consortium participation (with prior selection)
  • USB drive with proceedings
  • All coffee breaks and lunches
  • Welcome reception
  • Gala dinner (additional $40 to attend)

Valid full-time student ID required at check-in.

Payment Options

  • Credit Card. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay via IEEE secure payment gateway.
  • Bank Transfer. Wire transfer details supplied upon submission. Please include attendee name and registration ID in the wire reference.
