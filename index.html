About the Conference

A Premier IEEE Forum on Computational Finance & Business Intelligence

The International Conference on Business Intelligence and Financial Engineering (BIFE) has, since its inception in 2008, been one of Asia-Pacific's leading IEEE-sponsored venues for research at the intersection of data-driven business analytics and quantitative finance. After a multi-year hiatus, BIFE returns in 2024 under the technical sponsorship of the IEEE Computer Society.

BIFE 2024 is hosted in Hangzhou, a global hub for fintech innovation and home to leading institutions including Zhejiang University, Alibaba DAMO Academy, and Ant Group Research. The conference convenes academics, industry researchers, and policymakers around machine learning for finance, algorithmic trading, credit risk modeling, regulatory technology, and beyond.

All accepted papers are submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore and are indexed by EI Compendex, Scopus, and DBLP.

Technical Tracks

Topics of Interest

BIFE 2024 solicits original research across six technical tracks aligned with IEEE Computer Society scope.

TRACK 01

Machine Learning for Finance

Deep learning, reinforcement learning, neural networks for asset pricing, portfolio optimization, and predictive analytics.

TRACK 02

Financial Risk Management

Credit risk modeling, market risk, operational risk, systemic risk analysis, stress testing methodologies.

TRACK 03

Business Intelligence & Analytics

Data warehousing, OLAP, business analytics frameworks, decision support systems, knowledge discovery.

TRACK 04

Algorithmic & High-Frequency Trading

Market microstructure, execution algorithms, statistical arbitrage, limit order book modeling.

TRACK 05

FinTech & RegTech

Blockchain, distributed ledger, digital currencies, regulatory compliance automation, KYC/AML systems.

TRACK 06

Computational Economics

Agent-based modeling, behavioral finance, game theory, network analysis of financial systems.
