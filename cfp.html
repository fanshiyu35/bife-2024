Call for Papers

Submission guidelines for IEEE-format manuscripts

NOTE. Paper submission for BIFE 2024 closed on July 31, 2024. The list of accepted papers is published on the Accepted Papers page. This Call for Papers is preserved for archival reference.
Scope

Topics of Interest

BIFE 2024 invites original, previously unpublished research contributions across — but not limited to — the following technical areas:

A.1

Machine Learning for Finance

Deep learning, ensemble methods, reinforcement learning, transfer learning applied to asset pricing, portfolio construction, return prediction.

A.2

Credit & Default Risk

PD/LGD/EAD modeling, scoring, IFRS 9 / CECL implementation, credit scoring with alternative data.

A.3

Market Risk & VaR

Volatility forecasting, expected shortfall, copulas, stress testing, multivariate risk models.

A.4

Algorithmic Trading

Limit order book modeling, statistical arbitrage, market making, execution algorithms.

A.5

NLP for Finance

Sentiment analysis, news analytics, earnings call analysis, large language models in finance.

A.6

FinTech & Payments

Mobile payments, digital wallets, P2P lending, robo-advisors, embedded finance.

A.7

Blockchain & DeFi

DLT, smart contracts, decentralized finance, central-bank digital currencies (CBDC), tokenization.

A.8

RegTech & AML

Regulatory compliance automation, transaction monitoring, fraud detection, KYC, behavioral biometrics.

A.9

Business Intelligence

Data warehousing, OLAP, ETL, KPI dashboards, semantic layers, business analytics architectures.

A.10

Decision Support Systems

MCDM, fuzzy DSS, group decision making, knowledge-based DSS, expert systems for management.

A.11

Behavioral & Computational Economics

Agent-based simulation, evolutionary game theory, prospect theory in algorithms, market psychology.

A.12

Sustainable & ESG Finance

ESG scoring, climate-risk modeling, green bonds, sustainability-linked instruments.

Submission Guidelines

Manuscript Preparation & Submission

Format. All submissions must follow the official IEEE conference proceedings template (two-column, 10pt Times Roman). Use the IEEE templates available at ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html.

Length. Regular papers: 6–8 pages including references. A maximum of 2 additional pages may be added at a per-page fee at the camera-ready stage.

Originality. Submissions must be original work not concurrently under review elsewhere. IEEE CrossCheck similarity scans are applied to all accepted manuscripts.

Review. Double-blind peer review by at least three program committee members. Acceptance rate target: 35-40%.

Submission System. EasyChair — easychair.org/conferences/?conf=bife2024

IEEE PDF eXpress. Camera-ready PDFs must be validated through IEEE PDF eXpress prior to submission.
